Privacy Policy — Space Travel

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Space Travel ("the app") is a mobile arcade game developed by an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data collection

Space Travel does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no account system, no login, and no server backend.

Local data storage

The app stores your progress — high score, unlocked missions, in-game currency, and cosmetic/equipment choices — in a save file kept entirely on your own device (Android's private app storage). This data is never transmitted anywhere. It is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, or if you clear the app's data/storage from your device's app settings.

Advertising and analytics

The current version of Space Travel does not display third-party ads and does not include any analytics or tracking SDKs. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top of this page will reflect the change.

Third parties

Space Travel does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

Contact
