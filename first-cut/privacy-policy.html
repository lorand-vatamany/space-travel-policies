Privacy Policy — First Cut

Last updated: October 13, 2026

First Cut ("the app") is a single-player sword-duel game for Android, developed by an individual developer, Lorand Vatamany. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data collection

First Cut does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or device information. The app has no account system, no login, and no server backend, and it does not request any Android permissions, including internet access.

Local data storage

The app does not save any files on your device. Nothing about you or your games is kept after you close it.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

First Cut does not display ads, does not include any analytics or tracking SDKs, and has no in-app purchases. If that changes in a future update, this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top of this page will reflect the change.

Third parties

First Cut does not share any data with third parties, because it does not collect any data to share.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to vatamany.l@gmail.com.
